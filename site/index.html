playing-architect

I played architect with AI coding agents. Walk through the house the agents and I built.

Walk the house Two storeys and a roof terrace. About 1 million triangles, a 39 MB download.

Controls

Phone: drag the stick on the left to walk, drag on the right to look, tap the hand button to open doors and drawers.

Computer: W A S D or the arrow keys to walk, the mouse to look, E to open, M for the room menu, Tab to see it from outside.

How it was built three.js, Rapier, Blender, Python. Code and tests on GitHub
